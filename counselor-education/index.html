How it works

You'll meet with your professors and classmates once a week in a live, face-to-face video classroom. Real discussion, real relationships, real teaching, not recorded lectures you watch alone. Between sessions, you'll complete your reading and coursework on your own schedule.

Between classes, you'll connect with your classmates through short video conversations, seeing and hearing each other, not typing into the tired discussion boards you might expect.

It's the connection of a traditional classroom with the flexibility your life requires.

Two paths, one calling

Clinical Mental Health Counseling

If you feel called to walk with people through anxiety, trauma, addiction, and life's hardest seasons, this path prepares you for licensure as a professional counselor in agencies, private practice, and beyond.

School Counseling

If your heart is with children and teenagers, this path prepares you to become a licensed school counselor, guiding students through their academic, personal, and social growth.

Questions you're probably asking

Can I keep working while I'm in the program?

Yes. This program is built around working adults. You'll attend class by live video in the late afternoon or early evening, and complete your coursework on your own schedule.

How is the program structured, and how long does it take?

Classes run in focused eight-week terms, so you concentrate on one or two courses at a time instead of spreading yourself thin. Most students take two courses in the first term and one or two in the second, and finish in about two years.

When and how do classes meet?

Classes meet by live video, starting at either 4:00 or 6:00, for two to three hours per session. If you're taking two courses in a term, that's two evenings a week in class, so you always know exactly what your week looks like.

Where do I complete my practicum and internship?

In your own community, in schools, agencies, or clinical settings near you. Unlike your coursework, practicum and internship run across the full semester, giving you the sustained real-world experience that shapes you into a counselor.

Do I ever have to come to campus?

Your program is fully at a distance. You never relocate or commute to campus. We may ask you to visit campus once or twice over the course of your program for hands-on counseling skill development with your faculty and fellow students, always on a Saturday and announced well in advance.

When can I start?

You can begin in the fall, spring, or summer term.
